Hamari Pehli Anniversary ❤️

Har lafz mein tumhara zikr, har sher mein meri mohabbat...

Stanza 1 of 15
I • Pehla Pyaar

Ek saal pehle jo tumse pyaar hua tha,

Mere dil ko pehli baar tumse qaraar hua tha.

Kal tak jo zindagi bas yun hi guzar rahi thi,

Tumhare aane se har lamha gulzaar hua tha.

II • Meri Kainaat

Woh pehli mulaqaat, woh pehli si baat,

Tumhein sochkar muskurati meri har raat.

Pata hi nahi chala kab tum aadat ban gayi,

Kab meri subah, kab meri kainaat ban gayi.

III • Khwaab Sanwar Gaya

Dekhte-dekhte ek saal guzar gaya,

Mera dil tumhari mohabbat mein aur nikhar gaya.

Jo kal tak bas ek khwaab hua karti thi tum,

Aaj mera har khwaab tumse hi sanwar gaya.

IV • Meri Poori Duniya

Tumhari hansi mein mujhe bahaar milti hai,

Tumhari baaton mein dil ko qaraar milti hai.

Duniya bhale hi kitni khoobsurat kyun na ho,

Meri nazron ko bas tummein poori duniya milti hai.

V • Roothna Aur Manana

Kabhi roothna tumhara, kabhi pyaar se manana,

Kabhi chhoti si baat par mera tumhein satana.

Kabhi ghanton baatein, kabhi khamoshi mein izhaar,

Inhi chhoti-chhoti baaton mein basa hai apna pyaar.

VI • Ek Saal Ki Yaadein

Waqt bhaagta raha, hum yaadein banate rahe,

Har guzarte din ke saath aur kareeb aate rahe.

Calendar ke pannon ne ek saal poora kar diya,

Aur hum ek-dooje ko dil mein basaate rahe.

VII • Meri Dua Ho Tum ❤️

Smritu ji, tumhein shayad andaaza bhi nahi hoga,

Tumhare bina mera koi khwaab poora nahi hoga.

Tum sirf meri mohabbat nahi, meri dua bhi ho,

Mere chehre ki muskaan, mere dil ki wajah bhi ho.

VIII • Sukoon Ka Jahaan

Agar kabhi tumhari aankhon mein aansu aa jaayein,

Toh meri saari khushiyan tumhari muskaan ban jaayein.

Agar kabhi zindagi tumhein thakaakar rokne lage,

Meri baahon mein tumhein sukoon ka jahaan mil jaaye.

IX • Mera Waada

Main waada nahi karta ki har din bahaar hoga,

Na hi har pal hamare haq mein ye sansaar hoga.

Par itna zaroor hai, meri jaan, meri humdum,

Har mushkil mein tumhare saath mera pyaar hoga.

X • Barson Ka Safar

Na waqt ki raftaar se ye rishta kam hoga,

Na dooriyon ke aane se ye pyaar khatam hoga.

Aaj pehli anniversary hai, kal barson ka safar hoga,

Mere har aane wale kal mein tumhara hi zikr hoga.

XI • Dil Mein Ghar

Aaj hamari pehli saalgirah par bas itna kehna hai,

Tumhare saath hi mujhe apni har khushi mein rehna hai.

Tumse shuru hui kahaani tum par hi thehre,

Mujhe umr bhar tumhare dil mein apna ghar karna hai.

XII • Pehli Dhadkan

Ek saal pehle tumse mohabbat ka aagaaz hua,

Tumhari muskaan se mere dil ka har raaz hua.

Aaj bhi tumhein dekhkar wahi pehli dhadkan hoti hai,

Jaise kal hi tumse ishq hua, kal hi aagaaz hua.

XIII • Har Janam Mein Tum

Meri Smritu ji, bas itni si khwahish hai meri,

Har janam mein tum bano mohabbat meri.

Ye pehla saal toh bas shuruaat hai humaari,

Abhi toh umr bhar likhni hai kahaani humaari.

XIV • Hamesha Saath Chalna

Aur suno, meri jaan, ek baat yaad rakhna,

Mera haath thaamkar bas yun hi saath chalna.

Waqt badlega, mausam badlenge, badlegi duniya saari,

Par tumse mohabbat nahi badlegi, jaan-e-jaana humaari. ❤️
